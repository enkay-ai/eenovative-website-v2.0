Our home ground. Whole-school digital transformation, AI adoption, assessment and reporting systems, operations dashboards and digital literacy — designed and delivered inside real schools.

  • Reporting and assessment platforms

Professional services

Firms selling expertise lose margin to admin. We automate client onboarding, document workflows and reporting so billable people spend time on billable work.

Rotas, checklists, orders and customer follow-up — small operational leaks that compound daily. We digitise them without forcing staff through enterprise software.

  • Rota and admin support systems

Retail & e-commerce

Inventory in one system, orders in another, finance in a spreadsheet. We connect the pieces and give owners a single live view of the business.

  • CRM and customer data foundations

Healthcare administration

For clinics and care providers: the admin around care — scheduling, intake, compliance records — handled with security and data minimisation as first principles.

Construction & trades

Non-profits & community
