Who we help

Your situation,
not our services

Every engagement starts from where you are — your team, your constraints, your data — and works towards outcomes you can measure. Here's what that looks like for each kind of organisation we serve.

SMEs

Growing businesses

TYPICAL FIRST ENGAGEMENT: AUTOMATION SPRINT OR TECHNOLOGY AUDIT

Sound familiar?

  • The business runs on spreadsheets that only one person understands.
  • Staff spend hours each week on manual admin — chasing, re-typing, reconciling.
  • You can't answer "how are we doing this month?" without a day of digging.

What we deliver

  • Business process automation that removes repetitive work permanently.
  • A management dashboard with live KPIs leadership actually uses.
  • A pragmatic technology roadmap sized to your budget — not an enterprise fantasy.
Startups

New ventures

TYPICAL FIRST ENGAGEMENT: MVP BUILD OR ARCHITECTURE REVIEW

Sound familiar?

  • You need a working product before the funding conversation, not after.
  • Cloud costs and architecture choices feel like bets you can't evaluate.
  • Everyone says "add AI" — nobody says where it creates real value.

What we deliver

  • Rapid MVP development on sensible, scalable cloud architecture.
  • AI integration where it genuinely differentiates — with honest advice where it doesn't.
  • Foundations (identity, security, data) that won't need ripping out at Series A.
Schools

Education

TYPICAL FIRST ENGAGEMENT: DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION AUDIT

Sound familiar?

  • Reporting, assessment and admin consume evenings and weekends.
  • AI is arriving whether the school is ready or not — staff need confidence, not fear.
  • Systems don't talk to each other; data lives in silos and inboxes.

What we deliver

  • Whole-school digital transformation: workflows, reporting, communications, asset management.
  • Safe, practical AI adoption with staff training and clear governance.
  • Digital literacy programmes for staff, learners and the wider community.
Enterprise

Established organisations

TYPICAL FIRST ENGAGEMENT: DISCOVERY & PROCESS MAPPING

Sound familiar?

  • Departments outgrew their tools and now run on workarounds.
  • Leadership lacks a live view of operations — reporting is manual and late.
  • Legacy processes resist change because nobody owns the whole picture.

What we deliver

  • Internal systems, portals and business applications built around real workflows.
  • Executive dashboards and business intelligence with clean, validated data behind them.
  • Automation and consulting that modernises processes without disrupting delivery.
Public sector

Public sector & non-profits

TYPICAL FIRST ENGAGEMENT: SCOPING WORKSHOP

Sound familiar?

  • Digital transformation is mandated, but budgets and skills are stretched.
  • Security, accessibility and transparency requirements rule out quick fixes.
  • Communities you serve span every level of digital confidence.

What we deliver

  • Secure cloud migration and infrastructure with compliance thinking built in.
  • Transparent reporting and impact documentation funders can trust.
  • Digital inclusion and training programmes with accessible design as standard.
Not sure where you fit?

Describe your situation. We'll tell you honestly what would help.

Sometimes the answer is a two-week automation sprint, not a transformation programme. You'll get a straight recommendation either way.
