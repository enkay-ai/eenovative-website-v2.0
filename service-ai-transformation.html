What we deliver

Scope, in plain terms

FAQs

Fair questions

Will AI replace our staff?

In our engagements it removes tasks, not people — the repetitive fraction of roles. We design workflows where AI drafts and humans decide, which is where quality and trust both live.

What about data privacy?

Data minimisation is a design principle, not an afterthought. We define what data an AI system may see, prefer enterprise-grade tooling with appropriate data agreements, and document it all in your governance pack.

We tried a pilot and it fizzled. Why would this be different?

Most pilots die because success was never defined and nobody owned the workflow. We agree measurable criteria before building, and we build into real processes — not alongside them.
