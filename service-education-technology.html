How the engagement works

01

02

FAQs

Fair questions

Do you understand school data sensitivities?

Deeply — safeguarding-aware design, data minimisation and role-based access are defaults in everything we build for schools, and AI systems are scoped so pupil data stays protected.

Term time is chaos. When does work happen?

We plan around the school calendar: builds and migrations in holidays, training in INSET windows, pilots timed away from assessment pressure points.

Can you work with our existing MIS?

Usually yes — most of our school systems are designed to complement the MIS, handling the workflows it doesn't, rather than replacing it.
