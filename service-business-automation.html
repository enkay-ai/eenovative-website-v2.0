Is this you?

The signs we're usually called for

  • Staff re-type the same information into two or three different places.
  • Chasing people — for forms, approvals, returns, sign-offs — is somebody's actual job.
  • Month-end reporting means days of copy-paste from scattered spreadsheets.
  • Processes live in one person's head, and holidays are a risk event.
What we deliver

Scope, in plain terms

Workflow automation

Forms, sheets, documents, email and calendars connected into end-to-end workflows: request → approval → action → record, with every step logged. Built on the Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 tools you already pay for.

Document & report generation

Consistent, validated documents — reports, certificates, letters, invoices — generated from structured data instead of hand-assembled, eliminating both the labour and the copy-paste errors.

Automated reminders & alerts

Overdue items chase themselves. Escalations, renewal alerts and status notifications run on schedule, so nothing depends on someone remembering.

Live operational dashboards

Every automated workflow feeds a dashboard, so the process becomes visible: volumes, bottlenecks, overdue items and trends — updated continuously, not at month-end.

FAQs

Fair questions

Do we need new software licences?

Usually not. Most of our automation is built on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 platforms clients already own — which is also why it gets adopted: staff keep their familiar tools.

What happens when something breaks?

Every build ships with documentation, error alerting and a handover so your team can administer it. Ongoing support is available if you'd rather we keep watch.

How do you price an automation sprint?

Individually, after a discovery conversation — scoped to the workflow, not the hour. You'll always know the full cost before we start.
