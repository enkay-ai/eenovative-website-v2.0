Is this you?

The signs we're usually called for

  • Nobody is quite sure who has access to what — especially ex-staff.
  • Files live in personal drives, email attachments and three cloud accounts.
  • The CRM is a graveyard: half-adopted, out of date, quietly resented.
  • Security is 'probably fine', which is another way of saying unknown.
What we deliver

Scope, in plain terms

Cloud platform foundations

Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and AWS set up or restructured properly: organisational units, storage architecture, sharing policies, device posture and backup planning that reflects how you actually work.

Identity, access & security baseline

MFA enforcement, role-based access, offboarding procedures, incident basics and plain-language staff guidance — the controls that prevent the incidents that end up in the news.

Salesforce & CRM

Salesforce administration and development — objects, flows, dashboards, user management — or right-sized CRM alternatives when Salesforce is more than you need. Configured around your sales process, so it gets used.

Migration & consolidation

Moving between platforms or consolidating sprawl — planned, staged and communicated so the business keeps running while the ground moves underneath it.

FAQs

Fair questions

We're small. Do we really need this?

Small organisations are targeted precisely because attackers assume weak controls. The baseline — MFA, access control, backups — costs little and removes the most likely disasters. We'll size everything to your actual risk.

Salesforce or something simpler?

Honest answer: it depends on process complexity and budget. We configure Salesforce professionally, and we'll also tell you when a simpler CRM — or a structured sheet-based system — serves you better.

Can you take over from a previous provider?

Yes — audits often start exactly there. We document what exists before changing anything.
